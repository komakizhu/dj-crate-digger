# Brief mode

`composite` renders one final combined playlist. Use the selected locale's fixed report template and exactly twelve columns: title, artist, album/EP, style, BPM, key, duration, energy, release date, notes, selection reason, and link. Key follows BPM. Do not add a number, Mix, version, source, or verification column.

Use the global ranking contract with the user's explicit content priority. The pool goal is `min(max(2 × final_target, final_target + 8), 120)`. Verify every final row deeply, including complete title, direct allowed-platform link, dedupe key, and reliable key evidence when available. Unknown metadata remains unknown and does not displace a better candidate.

Arrange the set around the requested energy level or curve. Include the locale's digging-notes heading and exactly one compact creative transition suggestion. The suggestion must be tentative, actionable, and at most two techniques; metadata gaps do not justify omitting it.

After the complete report, render exactly three localized next-step items: natural-language feedback, text playlist/W4DJ handoff, and the optional harmonic-order action. A positive harmonic action loads [key-and-sequencing.md](../key-and-sequencing.md) and reorders this same combined collection only.
