# Key verification and sequencing

Load this resource only when a Brief or Rich report user gives an unambiguous positive harmonic-order response. Normal recommendation order follows the requested energy curve and does not claim harmonic mixing.

For Brief reorder the combined table. For Rich reorder only the dynamic-combined table; keep the style, scene, and familiarity/discovery views unchanged. Preserve the collection, complete titles, links, deduplication, artist cap, and transition suggestion.

## Key evidence

For Brief and Rich final tracks, a key may be accepted from a concrete page for the same complete title at an authorized DJ store or from explicit artist, label, or distributor metadata. Do not borrow a key between Original, Remix, Dub, Edit, Live, Instrumental, or other title variants. Conflicting sources, non-major/minor modes, and unknown values remain unknown and do not enter Camelot ordering.

## Order objective

Among known Camelot keys, prefer in order: maximize basic-compatible adjacent pairs; then maximize experimental same-letter ±2 links; then minimize breaks; then minimize normalized BPM movement; then preserve the original ranking. This is a practical heuristic, not a claim of mathematical optimality. Basic compatibility is the same code, same letter with number ±1, or same number with A/B switch. Experimental ±2 is a fallback only and must be labeled.

Unknown-key tracks remain in the result and go in the locale's awaiting-listening group at the end. Report experimental links and breaks briefly after the continuous table.

## Double drop candidates

Suggest at most five adjacent pairs. Both tracks must have reliable keys tied to their complete titles, basic Camelot compatibility, and half/double-normalized BPM difference no greater than 3%. Do not include experimental links. Every suggestion must say that the DJ still needs to audition phrasing, drums, melody, and the actual layered result; never claim to have auditioned or beatmatched it.
